Volley Clash 3D

Privacy overview

Applies to the Volley Clash 3D mobile application. Effective May 2026.

At a glance

This policy describes how Volley Clash 3D treats information on your device. The app is built so that routine gameplay does not rely on collecting personal profiles or building advertising dossiers about you.

Scope

These notices apply only to Volley Clash 3D when installed from the Apple App Store. They do not govern other apps, games, or services you may use on the same device.

On-device experience

Gameplay progress, match settings, and similar data may be stored locally on your device so the game can resume where you left off. That information stays under your control unless you delete the app or clear app data through your device settings.

Analytics & advertising

Volley Clash 3D is not designed to fingerprint your device for cross-app tracking. If Apple’s platform privacy labels indicate that no data is collected from the app, that reflects the developer’s current configuration as shown on the store listing.

Store disclosures can change when features update; check the App Store “App Privacy” section for the latest summary.

Platform & system services

Apple may process limited technical or billing information when you download, update, or make in-app purchases, according to Apple’s own policies. Crash diagnostics or performance data handled entirely by Apple’s operating system are governed by your device privacy settings, not by this page.

Children

The game is rated for broad audiences. Parents and guardians should use Screen Time and App Store parental controls to manage installs and purchases. The app is not intended to solicit personal details from players inside the match flow.

International use

Where privacy laws grant rights such as access or erasure, those rights are exercised primarily through the tools Apple provides for app data tied to your Apple ID, and through deleting the application from your devices.

Changes

This overview may be revised to stay aligned with product behavior and legal expectations. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are posted.
